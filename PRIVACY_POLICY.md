# Privacy Policy for Obfs Demo — Key Vault

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026  
**Application:** Obfs Demo — Key Vault (`com.demo.obfs_demo`)  

---

### Overview
Obfs Demo is an open-source technical demonstration and security testing application. **We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or user information.** All operations execute 100% offline and locally on your device.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect or request your name, email, phone number, physical address, or credentials.
- **No Tracking & Analytics:** We do not include third-party tracking libraries, telemetry SDKs, or analytics services.
- **No Advertisements:** The app contains no advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
- **Zero Remote Storage:** No databases or remote servers are contacted for logging, tracking, or profiling.

---

### 2. On-Device Signature & Cryptographic Operations
The application inspects the local Android package signing certificate (SHA-256 fingerprint) via the Android `PackageManager` API solely to demonstrate cryptographic signature-bound key decryption (AES-256-GCM). This diagnostic data:
- Exists strictly in transient device RAM.
- Is displayed on your device screen for your personal verification.
- Is never transmitted to external servers or third parties.

---

### 3. Device Permissions
The application does not request dangerous or privacy-invasive Android permissions:
- No Camera access
- No Microphone access
- No Location / GPS tracking
- No Contacts or Phone access

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age, complying with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

---

### 5. Third-Party Links & Services
This application does not link to third-party services that collect personal data.
